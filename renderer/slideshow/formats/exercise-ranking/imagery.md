# exercise-ranking Imagery

## Scope

This file owns the reusable visual interpretation of the designated `slideshow/exercise-ranking` reference and consumes `context/imagery.md`. It does not own copy, exercise claims, project coordinates, provider configuration, or production assets.

The designated evidence is the ordered eight-image post `7653231448740154644` under `references/`. The media is execution evidence only.

## Visual grammar

- Use full-bleed vertical candid gym or personal-record photography rather than white exercise cards or anatomy diagrams.
- Give each slide one recognizable subject: body-region context, exercise setup, or exercise-in-use view.
- Overlay one compact centered text stack with strong contrast and enough negative space to keep the decisive body or equipment detail visible.
- Keep typography stable, but derive exact placement from each image rather than copying coordinates.
- Keep essential text away from TikTok controls and the lower interface zone.

## Capture carriers

Preserve the reference's heterogeneous personal-record function: plausible self-shots, openly shared training-partner angles, seated equipment POV, and equipment-detail views. Use ordinary gym light, imperfect framing, equipment clutter, and non-performative posture. The camera's holder and purpose should be plausible.

Show enough machine, bench, limb position, grip, and body orientation to distinguish the setup. Reject body-only outcome proof, voyeuristic framing, commercial posing, polished advertising light, synthetic anatomy, and impossible camera causality.

## Sequence

- **Cover:** direct body-region or equipment context supporting the comparison without implying transformation proof.
- **Item slides:** one exercise-specific candid image per ranked item with enough setup evidence.
- **Personal context:** only truthful first-party evidence when separately supported.
- **Product insert:** excluded during prelaunch.

Keep contrast, hierarchy, and mobile-safe margins consistent while varying exposure, camera height, carrier, cast, location, and equipment angle. Remove TikTok UI, source branding, watermarks, and unrelated product screens from adaptations.
